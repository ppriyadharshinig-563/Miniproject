Exact matching with Student.java and Main.java

boolean isEligibleToBoard = student.isTransportAuthorized() && student.isPresent() && student.isBusRequired();

A student is qualified to board today's bus only if all three conditions evaluate to TRUE:

  • 1. Transport Authorized: Approved annual pass and fee clearance recorded.
  • 2. Campus Attendance: Marked physically present for today's session.
  • 3. Travel Intention: Declared requirement for college bus commute tomorrow.
